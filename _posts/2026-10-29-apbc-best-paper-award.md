We congratulate Tin Pang, the recipient of the APBC 2026 Best Paper Award!

This prestigious award recognizes Tin Pang’s innovative workflow for integrating dynamic DMI data with genome-scale metabolic modeling (GEM).  
Bridging noninvasive tracer imaging with network-level metabolic inference, his work advances our ability to investigate tissue-specific metabolic activity through adequately identified signals.  
The workflow incorporates shared plasma input, tissue-specific kinetics, voxel-level fluxes, isotope effects, and measurement uncertainty, providing a comprehensive framework for connecting imaging data with metabolic modeling.  

Congratulations to Tin Pang on this great achievement
